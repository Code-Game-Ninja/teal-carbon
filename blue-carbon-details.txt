Yes. I researched the **actual Sapphire Project → Blue Carbon Lab page**, its current site structure, and the broader Sapphire design system. The page is essentially a **premium environmental-impact storytelling page**: large photography, editorial typography, lots of whitespace, restrained navigation, and a strong “science → impact → action” narrative. ([The Sapphire Project][1])

## 1. What the page is doing

The Blue Carbon Lab page has a very simple information architecture:

1. **Global navigation**

   * About
   * Initiatives
   * Impact
   * Donate
2. **Hero / quote**

   * Large statement about blue carbon
   * Scientist attribution
3. **Profile**

   * What Blue Carbon Lab is
4. **Our Work**

   * Explanation of blue-carbon science
5. **Sapphire Impact**

   * What the funding/project enables
6. **External CTA**

   * Visit Blue Carbon Lab
7. **Trust / organization information**
8. **Footer / acknowledgment** ([The Sapphire Project][1])

The important thing is that it **doesn't feel like a normal corporate website**. It feels closer to a **digital editorial/magazine experience**.

---

# 2. The visual design

### Overall style

I'd describe the design direction as:

> **Luxury editorial + environmental documentary + scientific storytelling**

The broader Sapphire site uses large imagery, short editorial sections, numbered sections, oversized headings and project cards. ([The Sapphire Project][2])

For your site, I would use:

* Deep ocean blue
* Almost-black navy
* Off-white / warm white
* Muted sand
* Sea-green accent
* Very subtle gradients
* Large photographic backgrounds
* Thin borders
* Minimal shadows

### Suggested palette

```text
#071B22  → Deep Ocean
#0D3038  → Dark Teal
#123F48  → Ocean Teal
#DCE9E5  → Mist
#F5F3ED  → Warm White
#B8C8BD  → Sage
#FFFFFF  → White
```

Avoid the typical:

```text
bright blue
bright green
neon gradients
huge rounded cards
```

That would make it look like a generic climate-tech startup rather than the reference.

# 3. Typography

Typography is **one of the biggest parts of the look**.

I'd recommend pairing:

### Heading

**Cormorant Garamond / DM Serif Display / Playfair Display**

with:

### Body

**Inter / Manrope / Neue Montreal-style font**

Example:

```text
BLUE CARBON LAB

Researching the coastline.
Restoring what protects it.
```

Large serif heading + clean sans-serif body gives you that editorial feeling.

# 5. The storytelling should be scroll-driven

This is probably the biggest thing I'd copy conceptually from Sapphire.

Don't make:

```text
Hero
↓
About
↓
Services
↓
Contact
```

Instead make the user feel like they're **moving through a story**.

For example:

### 01 — THE PROBLEM

> Coastal ecosystems are disappearing.

Large image.

Small supporting statistics.

↓

### 02 — THE SCIENCE

> Blue carbon ecosystems capture and store carbon.

Interactive ecosystem visualization.

↓

### 03 — THE RESEARCH

Show:

```text
Mangroves
Saltmarshes
Seagrass
Tidal wetlands
```

↓

### 04 — THE IMPACT

Large numbers:

```text
51,700 ha
potential restoration area

115,596
tCO₂e / year

1,391
properties
```

These figures are examples from the Queensland Blue research reported by Sapphire; if you build your own site, use your project's verified numbers rather than copying them. ([The Sapphire Project][3])

↓

### 05 — THE FUTURE

Full-screen image/video.

```text
Science can tell us
where to begin.

Restoration is how
we move forward.
```

# 7. Image treatment

Photography is extremely important.

Don't use random stock images.

Use:

* aerial mangrove shots
* underwater seagrass
* researchers in the field
* soil/core sampling
* satellite imagery
* coastline drone footage
* scientists working
* community restoration
* macro ecosystem photography

The Sapphire page itself uses a large environmental image and scientific/organizational imagery rather than relying purely on UI elements. ([The Sapphire Project][1])

You can also mix:

```text
FULL BLEED IMAGE
      ↓
small caption
      ↓
editorial text
```

rather than putting every image inside a rounded card.

# 13. Micro-interactions

This site would benefit massively from subtle animations.

### On page load

```text
Image → slowly reveals
Heading → slides upward
Navigation → fades in
```

### On scroll

```text
Images → parallax
Text → fade/translate
Numbers → count upward
Sections → reveal
```

### Cursor

You could create a custom cursor:

```text
          ◉
      EXPLORE
```

When hovering an image:

```text
      VIEW PROJECT →
```

Keep these subtle.

## 18. One thing I would NOT copy

Don't clone the Sapphire site pixel-for-pixel.

Use it as a **design reference**, but create your own:

* typography
* visual identity
* copy
* photography
* animations
* information architecture
* colors
* illustrations

The Sapphire page is presenting **Deakin University's Blue Carbon Lab through Sapphire's impact/fundraising narrative**, rather than functioning as the full research lab's own website. ([The Sapphire Project][1])

So your version can actually go much further by combining the **editorial luxury of Sapphire** with a **real scientific/research interface**.

### My recommended design direction

**Sapphire-inspired + scientific + cinematic + interactive**

```text
             CINEMATIC
                 │
                 ▼
        ┌─────────────────┐
        │  LARGE IMAGERY  │
        └────────┬────────┘
                 │
                 ▼
       EDITORIAL TYPOGRAPHY
                 │
                 ▼
          DATA / SCIENCE
                 │
                 ▼
       INTERACTIVE MAPS
                 │
                 ▼
        RESEARCH PROJECTS
                 │
                 ▼
           REAL IMPACT
```

If you're going to actually build this, I would use **React + Tailwind + GSAP + Lenis + Mapbox**, with a dark ocean/cream editorial design rather than a conventional SaaS layout. The result can look extremely premium while still being practical for a research organization.
