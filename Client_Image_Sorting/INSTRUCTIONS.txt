========================================================================
TEAL CARBON LAB - IMAGE SORTING INSTRUCTIONS
========================================================================

Hello! To get the website ready, we need your help picking the best 
photos for the new Teal Carbon Lab website. 

All of your raw images have been placed in the "Unsorted_Images" folder. 
Please review them and drag-and-drop the best ones into the appropriate 
categorised folders (numbered 1 through 4).

Here is a guide on what we need for each folder:

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1_Hero_and_Headers
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT'S FOR: The big, impactful images at the very top of each page 
(Home, About, Research, etc.).
WHAT TO LOOK FOR: 
 - High-resolution, wide (landscape) orientation.
 - Sweeping landscapes, beautiful drone shots of wetlands, or highly 
   cinematic nature shots.
 - Nothing too busy in the center so that text can be read over it.
HOW MANY: Pick your absolute best 5 to 10 images.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2_Projects_and_Ecosystems
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT'S FOR: The sections detailing specific ecosystems (Marshes, 
Swamps, Peatlands, etc.) and active projects (e.g., Keoladeo, Sambhar Lake).
WHAT TO LOOK FOR: 
 - Distinctive shots that clearly represent specific ecosystems.
 - Images showing fieldwork, carbon measurement, or local environments.
HOW MANY: Around 10 to 15 images. 

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3_Team_and_Researchers
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT'S FOR: The "About" page and team profiles.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR: 
 - Clear, professional, or active fieldwork portraits of the team.
 - Ensure faces are visible and well-lit. 
HOW MANY: 1 profile picture per team member + a few group shots.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4_General_Gallery
------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT'S FOR: The masonry gallery page and miscellaneous filler images.
WHAT TO LOOK FOR:
 - A good mix of everything: landscapes, action shots of researchers, 
   wildlife, macro shots of flora/fauna, etc.
HOW MANY: Pick the best 20 to 30 images to showcase the lab's work. 
(Any more than 30 might slow down the website).


WHAT HAPPENS NEXT?
===================
Once you have moved the files from "Unsorted_Images" into the numbered 
folders, send this entire "Client_Image_Sorting" folder back to the 
development team (via ZIP, Google Drive, or WeTransfer). We will take it 
from there, compress the images for web performance, and integrate them 
straight into the website!

Thank you!
